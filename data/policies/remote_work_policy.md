# Remote Work Policy

- **Policy ID:** RW-001
- **Version:** 2.0
- **Effective date:** 2025-07-01
- **Owner:** People Operations

## RW-1 Purpose and Scope

This policy sets out how employees of Halvorsen Analytics may work remotely while keeping teams productive, connected, and secure. It applies to all employees. Contractors follow the remote-work terms in their individual agreements. Remote work is a privilege tied to role suitability and performance, not an entitlement, and it may be adjusted when business needs change.

## RW-2 Work Arrangements

Halvorsen Analytics recognises three arrangements:

- **Office-based:** the employee works on-site at least four days per week.
- **Hybrid:** the employee works on-site on **two anchor days per week**, Tuesday and Thursday, and remotely on the other days.
- **Fully remote:** the employee works remotely and visits the office for planned team events at least once per quarter.

Hybrid is the default arrangement for most roles. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department director and People Operations.

## RW-3 Eligibility

Employees become eligible for hybrid or fully remote work after completing their **90-day probation period**. Roles that require handling physical equipment, on-site customer visits, or restricted data rooms may be designated office-based. Employees on a formal performance improvement plan may have their remote arrangement paused for the duration of the plan.

## RW-4 Requesting an Arrangement

To request a hybrid or fully remote arrangement, submit **form HR-RW-04** in the HR portal. The request must describe the proposed schedule, the home workspace, and how the employee will stay reachable during core hours. Managers must respond within ten business days. Approved arrangements are reviewed every twelve months or whenever the employee changes role.

## RW-5 Core Hours and Availability

All employees, regardless of arrangement, must be available between **10:00 and 15:00 in their local time zone** on working days. During core hours employees should respond to messages within one hour and keep their calendar status accurate. Meetings involving multiple time zones should be scheduled within the overlap of participants' core hours wherever possible.

## RW-6 Home Office Setup

Remote and hybrid employees receive a **one-time home office stipend of $500** to buy ergonomic furniture or accessories. The stipend is claimed through the expense system and requires itemized receipts. The company provides a laptop, monitor, keyboard, and headset; this equipment remains company property and must be returned when employment ends. Employees are responsible for a reliable internet connection of at least 50 Mbps download. A monthly internet allowance of $40 is paid through payroll to fully remote employees only.

## RW-7 Working From Another Location

Employees may work from a location other than their registered home address for up to **20 working days per calendar year** without additional approval, provided the location is in the same country. Working from another country, even briefly, requires prior approval from People Operations and Legal because of tax, immigration, and data-protection obligations. Unapproved international remote work may result in the arrangement being withdrawn.

## RW-8 Security While Working Remotely

Remote employees must follow the IT Security Policy (SEC-001) at all times. In particular, company data may only be accessed from company-managed devices, the VPN must be used on any network that is not the employee's own home network, and screens must be locked whenever the device is unattended. Printed confidential material must not leave the office.

## RW-9 Health, Safety, and Wellbeing

Employees are responsible for keeping their home workspace safe. Work-related injuries at home must be reported to People Operations within 24 hours. Managers should hold a one-to-one at least every two weeks with each remote team member, and employees are encouraged to take regular breaks and to disconnect outside working hours.

## RW-10 Ending or Changing an Arrangement

Either the employee or the manager may propose ending a remote arrangement with **30 days' written notice**. Shorter notice applies only where there is a serious performance or security concern. Changes to the arrangement are recorded in the HR portal against the original HR-RW-04 request.
