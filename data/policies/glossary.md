# Glossary

Terms and abbreviations used across Halvorsen Analytics policies and guides, in alphabetical order.

**Anchor days** — The two days per week (Tuesday and Thursday) when hybrid employees work on-site. See RW-001.

**Blackout period** — Dates when a team normally does not approve PTO, such as Finance's quarter-end close. Published in the team calendar at least 60 days ahead. See PTO-001.

**Carryover** — Unused PTO days moved into the next calendar year; at most 5 days, which expire on 31 March. See PTO-001.

**Core hours** — 10:00–15:00 local time, when every employee must be available on working days. See RW-001.

**Daily meal limit** — The maximum claimable meal spend per day of overnight business travel, currently $75. It is a cap on actual receipted spend, not a per diem. See EXP-001.

**FIN-REQ-17** — The expense claim form in the expense system, used for all reimbursement claims and learning-budget purchases.

**HR-RW-04** — The HR portal form for requesting a hybrid or fully remote working arrangement.

**LV-PTO** — The Leave Request option in the HR portal used to book paid time off.

**MFA** — Multi-factor authentication. Mandatory for email, the VPN, the HR portal, the expense system and all production systems. See SEC-001.

**Missing Receipt Declaration** — A form, countersigned by your manager, that replaces a lost receipt. More than three in a calendar year triggers a Finance review.

**Probation period** — The first 90 days of employment, after which employees become eligible for remote arrangements.

**Replacement day** — A day off given for working on a public holiday or during the year-end shutdown; must be taken within 60 days.

**Restricted data** — The highest data classification: credentials, encryption keys and regulated personal data. Must be encrypted at rest and in transit. See SEC-001.

**Severity 1 (Sev 1)** — The most serious incident level, e.g. a confirmed breach of Restricted data; escalated to the CISO within 15 minutes. See SEC-001.

**Superseded policy** — An older version of a policy that has been replaced by a newer version with the same policy ID. Only the version with the latest effective date applies.

**VPN** — The company virtual private network, required on any network other than the office network or your own home network.
