# Employee Benefits Overview

This page summarises the benefits available to full-time employees of Halvorsen Analytics. Part-time employees working at least 20 hours per week receive the same benefits on a pro-rata basis unless a row below says otherwise. Contractors and interns are not covered.

Detailed rules live in the individual policies; where a benefit is governed by another policy, the policy ID is listed so you can check the full text.

## At a glance

| Benefit | What you get | When it starts | Governed by |

| Health insurance | Medical cover for you and dependants; company pays 85% of the premium | First day of employment | BEN-001 |
| Dental and vision | Basic dental and vision plan; company pays 100% for employees, 50% for dependants | First day of employment | BEN-001 |
| Retirement plan | Company matches contributions up to **4% of base salary** | After 30 days | BEN-001 |

| Internet allowance | **$40 per month**, fully remote employees only | After arrangement is approved | RW-001 |
| Learning budget | **$1,000 per calendar year** for courses, books and conferences | After 90 days | BEN-001 |
| Wellness allowance | **$50 per month** towards gym, sports or mental-health apps | After 90 days | BEN-001 |

## Learning budget

The learning budget can be spent on anything that builds skills relevant to your role or your next role: online courses, certification exams, technical books, and conference tickets. Conference travel is booked and reimbursed under the Expense Reimbursement Policy (EXP-001), not from the learning budget. Unused learning budget does not roll over into the next calendar year. Request approval from your manager before spending, then claim through the expense system using form FIN-REQ-17 with the category "Learning".

## Wellness allowance

The wellness allowance is paid through payroll each month once you have submitted a single wellness declaration in the HR portal. You do not need to submit receipts each month, but People Operations may ask for evidence during an annual review.

## Retirement plan

Employees can contribute any percentage of salary up to the legal limit. The company match is calculated on base salary only, not bonuses, and vests immediately. Changes to your contribution rate can be made at any time in the benefits portal and take effect from the next payroll run.

## Changes to your benefits

You can change health and dental elections during the open enrollment window each November, or within 30 days of a qualifying life event such as marriage, the birth or adoption of a child, or a dependant losing other cover. Contact People Operations through the HR portal for help with any benefit question.
