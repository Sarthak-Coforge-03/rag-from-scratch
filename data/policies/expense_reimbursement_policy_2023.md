# Expense Reimbursement Policy

- **Policy ID:** EXP-001
- **Version:** 2.1
- **Effective date:** 2023-03-01
- **Owner:** Finance Operations

## EXP-1 Purpose and Scope

This policy explains how Halvorsen Analytics reimburses employees for reasonable, necessary business expenses paid with personal funds. It applies to all full-time and part-time employees, interns, and contractors whose agreements explicitly include expense reimbursement. Expenses must be directly related to company business and must not be lavish or extravagant. When in doubt, ask your manager before you spend.

## EXP-2 General Principles

Employees are expected to spend company money as if it were their own. The lowest reasonable cost option should be chosen when it does not compromise safety or the business purpose of the trip. Personal expenses, including entertainment for family members, personal travel extensions, fines, and loyalty-program upgrades, are never reimbursable. Splitting a single expense across multiple claims to avoid an approval threshold is a policy violation.

## EXP-3 Travel

Air travel must be booked through the company travel portal in economy class for all flights regardless of duration. Rail travel is preferred over flights for trips under three hours. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to the nightly rate published for each city in the travel portal; stays above that rate require written justification. Mileage for personal vehicles is reimbursed at the current government standard rate, and employees must record the start and end locations for each trip. Ride-share and taxi fares are reimbursable for business travel. Airport parking is reimbursed in full for trips of up to five days.

## EXP-4 Meals

### EXP-4.1 Business Meals with Clients

Meals with clients or prospects are reimbursable when a business discussion takes place. The claim must list the attendees, their company, and the business purpose. Alcohol is reimbursable only at client meals and is limited to two drinks per person.

### EXP-4.2 Meal Allowances

When traveling overnight for business, employees may claim meals up to a **daily meal limit of $50 per day**, covering breakfast, lunch, and dinner combined. The limit is a cap, not a per diem: only actual spend backed by receipts is reimbursed. Meals already provided by a conference, hotel, or airline should not be claimed. Same-day travel without an overnight stay does not qualify for a meal allowance.

## EXP-5 Receipts and Documentation

Itemized receipts are required for **any single expense over $25**. Expenses of $25 or less may be claimed without a receipt but must still include the date, vendor, amount, and business purpose. Credit card statements are not accepted as a substitute for itemized receipts. If a receipt is lost, the employee must complete a Missing Receipt Declaration, which their manager must countersign.

## EXP-6 Submitting a Claim

All claims are submitted through the expense system using **form FIN-REQ-17**. Claims must be submitted **within 60 days** of the date the expense was incurred. Claims submitted after 60 days require written approval from a director and may be rejected. For trips, submit a single claim covering the whole trip within 60 days of your return date. Foreign currency expenses are converted at the exchange rate on the transaction date shown on your card statement.

## EXP-7 Approvals

Every claim must be approved by the employee's direct manager. Claims totaling more than $1,000 additionally require approval from the department director. Managers may not approve their own expenses; those claims go to the next level of management. Finance Operations performs random audits on approved claims each quarter.

## EXP-8 Reimbursement Timing

Approved claims are paid by direct deposit in the next scheduled payroll run, provided the claim was approved at least five business days before the payroll cutoff. Employees can track the status of each claim in the expense system. Questions about a specific claim should be sent to Finance Operations with the claim number in the subject line.

## EXP-9 Policy Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Chief Financial Officer. Approved exceptions must be attached to the claim in FIN-REQ-17. This policy is reviewed every two years by Finance Operations.
