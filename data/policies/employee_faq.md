# Employee FAQ

Answers to the questions People Operations, Finance and IT get asked most often. If your question isn't here, post it in #ask-people-ops and we'll add it.

**Q: How much can I spend on meals when I travel for work?**
When you stay overnight on a business trip you can claim actual meal costs up to $75 per day for breakfast, lunch and dinner combined. Don't claim meals that the conference or hotel already provided. A same-day trip only qualifies if it lasts more than ten hours.

**Q: How long do I have to submit an expense claim?**
30 days from the date you paid. For a trip, submit one claim covering everything within 30 days of getting back. Late claims need a director's sign-off and can be rejected.

**Q: Do I need receipts for everything?**
Only for single expenses over $50. Smaller items just need the date, vendor, amount and business purpose. If you lose a receipt, fill in a Missing Receipt Declaration and get your manager to countersign it.

**Q: Which form do I use for expenses?**
Everything goes through form FIN-REQ-17 in the expense system, including learning-budget purchases.

**Q: How many days of PTO can I carry into next year?**
Up to 5 unused days. They must be used by 31 March or they expire.

**Q: How far ahead do I have to book time off?**
Five working days ahead for up to three days off; three weeks ahead for four or more consecutive days. Use the Leave Request (LV-PTO) option in the HR portal.

**Q: Can I work from home?**
Most roles are hybrid: in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays and remote the rest of the week. You become eligible once your 90-day probation ends. Apply with form HR-RW-04.

**Q: What are core hours?**
10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Everyone, wherever they work, should be reachable during those hours.

**Q: Can I work from my parents' place for a couple of weeks?**
Yes, if it's in the same country — you can work from somewhere other than your home address for up to 20 working days a year without extra approval. Working from another country always needs approval from People Operations and Legal first.

**Q: Do I need the VPN at home?**
Not on your own home network. You do need it on any other network: hotels, airports, cafés, a friend's Wi-Fi.

**Q: I clicked a link in a suspicious email. What now?**
Report it within one hour in #sec-incident or call the incident hotline. Don't delete the email and don't try to investigate it yourself.

**Q: How long does my password need to be?**
At least 14 characters, stored in the company password manager, and never reused across systems.

**Q: When do I get paid back for expenses?**
In the next payroll run, as long as your claim was approved at least five business days before the payroll cutoff.
