At Halvorsen Analytics we build products that customers trust with their data, and that trust starts with how we treat each other and the people we work with. This Code of Conduct describes the behaviour we expect from every employee, contractor and intern, in the office, online, at customer sites and at company events. It does not try to list every situation. When something isn't covered, ask yourself whether you would be comfortable explaining your choice to your manager, to a customer, and to a journalist. If the answer to any of those is no, stop and ask for advice.

We treat everyone with respect. Harassment, bullying and discrimination on the basis of race, colour, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, national origin or any other protected characteristic are never acceptable. This includes jokes, comments and messages that might seem harmless to the sender but make colleagues feel unwelcome. Disagreement about work is healthy; personal attacks are not. Managers carry an extra responsibility to model this behaviour and to act quickly when they see it break down.

We deal honestly. Records, expense claims, timesheets and reports must be accurate and complete. Splitting a purchase to stay under an approval limit, claiming personal costs as business expenses, or backdating a document are all serious breaches, even when the amounts are small. We don't offer or accept bribes, and gifts or hospitality from customers and suppliers must be modest, infrequent and declared to Legal if their value is more than $100. Anything that could look like it influences a business decision should be declined politely.

We avoid conflicts of interest. You must tell your manager and Legal about any outside job, investment, family relationship or board role that could affect, or appear to affect, your judgement at work. Declaring a possible conflict early is never a problem; hiding one is.

We protect information. Customer data, financial results and unreleased product plans are confidential, and credentials and personal data are restricted. Follow the IT Security Policy at all times, never paste company data into tools that aren't approved, and report anything that looks like a security incident within one hour.

We speak up. If you see something that breaks this Code, the law, or another company policy, raise it with your manager, People Operations, Legal, or through the confidential ethics hotline, which accepts anonymous reports. Halvorsen Analytics does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith, even if the concern turns out to be unfounded. Retaliation is itself a breach of this Code.

## Consequences

Breaches of this Code are taken seriously and may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract, and may be reported to the authorities where the law requires it. Every employee confirms each year that they have read and will follow this Code as part of their annual compliance training.
