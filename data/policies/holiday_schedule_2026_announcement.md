From: People Operations

Date: 8 December 2025
Subject: 2026 holiday calendar and year-end office closure

Hi everyone,

The 2026 public holiday calendar is now published in the HR portal under Time Off → Holiday Calendar. As always, Halvorsen Analytics observes the public holidays of the country you are employed in, so your calendar may look different from that of colleagues in other offices. Please check yours now and add the dates to your team calendar, especially if you manage a team that spans several countries and needs cover on days when only some locations are closed.

A few reminders that come up every year:

Public holidays don't count against your PTO balance. If your manager asks you to work on a public holiday — for example to support a customer launch — you'll receive a replacement day off, which must be taken within 60 days of the holiday. Agree the replacement day with your manager in advance and record it in the HR portal so it isn't lost.

This year the company will again close all offices for the year-end shutdown, from 24 December 2026 to 1 January 2027 inclusive. The shutdown days that are not public holidays in your country are given as extra paid days off and are not deducted from your PTO. A small on-call rota from IT and Security Operations will cover incidents during the shutdown; if you are on the rota you will receive replacement days under the same 60-day rule.

If you are planning longer time off around the holidays, remember that requests for four or more consecutive days need to go in at least three weeks ahead through Leave Request (LV-PTO), and that up to five unused PTO days can be carried into 2027 but must be used by 31 March. The Finance team's quarter-end blackout still applies in late December, so Finance colleagues should check with their manager before booking.

Thanks, and we hope you all get a proper rest at the end of the year.

People Operations
