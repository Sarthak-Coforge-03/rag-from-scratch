# How to book business travel

A step-by-step guide for booking and claiming a business trip. It follows the Expense Reimbursement Policy (EXP-001); if this guide and the policy ever disagree, the policy wins.

### Before you book

1. Check the trip is necessary. A video call is usually cheaper and faster; travel when meeting in person clearly matters.
2. Get your manager's approval for the trip in writing (a message in the team channel is fine) and note the business purpose — you'll need it for the claim.
3. If the trip goes to another country, check whether you need a visa and tell People Operations if you plan to work remotely before or after the business days.

### Booking transport

4. Book everything through the company travel portal. Bookings made elsewhere may not be reimbursed.
5. For trips under three hours, choose rail rather than flying.
6. Flights under six hours are economy class. For flights of six hours or more you may book premium economy, but only with your manager's approval.
7. Use ride-share or taxis for business travel where public transport is impractical. If you drive to the airport, parking is reimbursed only up to the cost of a round-trip ride-share fare, so a ride-share is often the simpler choice.
8. Driving your own car? Mileage is paid at the government standard rate. Record start and end locations for every leg.

### Booking a hotel

9. Pick a hotel at or below the nightly rate the travel portal shows for that city.
10. If nothing suitable is available at that rate, you can book above it — but add a written justification to the booking notes.

### During the trip

11. Keep itemized receipts for anything over $50. Card statements don't count as receipts.
12. Meals are covered up to $75 per day when you stay overnight. Skip any meal the hotel, airline or conference already provided.
13. Client dinners are claimed separately as business meals: list who attended, their company and what you discussed. Alcohol is limited to two drinks per person.

### After the trip

14. Submit one claim for the whole trip on form FIN-REQ-17 within 30 days of your return date.
15. Attach every receipt, plus a Missing Receipt Declaration countersigned by your manager for anything you lost.
16. If the total is over $2,500, your claim also needs your department director's approval, so allow extra time.
17. Track the claim in the expense system. Approved claims are paid in the next payroll run if approved at least five business days before the cutoff.

Questions about a booking go to the travel portal help desk; questions about a claim go to Finance Operations.
