Welcome to Halvorsen Analytics! This guide walks you through your first three months. Work through the checklist with your manager and your onboarding buddy; tick items off in the HR portal as you go.

## Before day one

- [ ] Sign your contract and return the tax and bank-detail forms in the HR portal
- [ ] Confirm your delivery address so IT can ship your laptop, monitor, keyboard and headset
- [ ] Read the Employee Benefits Overview so you can choose health cover on day one

## Week 1

- [ ] Collect or unbox your laptop; it arrives already enrolled in device management
- [ ] Set up the company password manager and create a password of at least 14 characters
- [ ] Turn on multi-factor authentication for email, the VPN, the HR portal and the expense system
- [ ] Join #ask-people-ops, #sec-incident and your team channel
- [ ] Meet your manager to agree goals for your 90-day probation period
- [ ] Choose your health, dental and retirement elections in the benefits portal

## Within 14 days

- [ ] Complete security awareness training — it is mandatory within 14 days of joining
- [ ] Read the IT Security Policy, especially data classification and incident reporting
- [ ] Book a session with Finance Operations if your role involves travel or purchasing

## First month

- [ ] Shadow at least two team meetings and one customer call, if your role is customer-facing
- [ ] Have a one-to-one with your manager every week
- [ ] After your first 30 days you can start using accrued PTO; requests go through Leave Request (LV-PTO)

## Months two and three

- [ ] Take ownership of a first small project agreed with your manager
- [ ] Mid-probation check-in with your manager and People Operations around day 45
- [ ] End-of-probation review around day 90; once you pass, you can request a hybrid or fully remote arrangement with form HR-RW-04

## Things people often miss

Your laptop and accessories are company property and must be returned when you leave. Don't install software that isn't on the approved list — ask the IT Service Desk instead. If you travel in your first months, keep every receipt over $50 and submit your claim within 30 days of returning.

## Who to ask

Your onboarding buddy is your first stop for "how do we do things here" questions. Benefits, contracts and leave questions go to People Operations; laptop, access and password problems go to the IT Service Desk; expense questions go to Finance Operations with the claim number in the subject line.
