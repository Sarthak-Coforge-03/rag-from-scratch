# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

- **Policy ID:** PTO-001
- **Version:** 4.0
- **Effective date:** 2026-01-01
- **Owner:** People Operations

## PTO-1 Purpose and Scope

This policy explains how employees of Halvorsen Analytics earn, request, and use paid time off. It applies to all full-time employees. Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rata basis according to their contracted hours. Interns and contractors are covered by the terms of their individual agreements and are not eligible for the PTO described here.

## PTO-2 Annual Entitlement

Full-time employees receive PTO based on their length of continuous service:

- **Less than 3 years of service:** 20 days per year
- **3 to 5 years of service:** 23 days per year
- **More than 5 years of service:** 25 days per year

The increase takes effect on the first day of the month following the service anniversary. PTO covers vacation and personal time; sick leave and public holidays are handled separately and do not reduce the PTO balance.

## PTO-3 Accrual

PTO accrues monthly at one twelfth of the annual entitlement, credited on the last working day of each month. New employees begin accruing from their start date, and may use accrued PTO after their first 30 days of employment. Employees may borrow up to five days against future accrual with manager approval; any borrowed days that have not been accrued are deducted from final pay if employment ends.

## PTO-4 Requesting Time Off

PTO must be requested in the HR portal using the **Leave Request (code LV-PTO)** option. Requests of up to three consecutive days must be submitted at least **five working days** in advance. Requests of four or more consecutive days must be submitted at least **three weeks** in advance. Managers must approve or decline within five working days. Requests are generally approved on a first-come, first-served basis, but managers may decline requests that would leave a team without adequate coverage.

## PTO-5 Carryover

Employees may carry over a maximum of **5 unused PTO days** into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March** of the following year, after which they expire. Unused days above the carryover limit expire on 31 December. Exceptions are granted only where an employee was prevented from taking leave by a documented business requirement, and must be approved by the department director and People Operations.

## PTO-6 Blackout Periods

Some teams have blackout periods during which PTO is normally not approved. The Finance team's blackout period runs from the last five working days of each quarter through the third working day of the next quarter, to support financial close. Other teams may define blackout periods by publishing them in the team calendar at least 60 days in advance. Emergency leave during a blackout period is handled under PTO-8.

## PTO-7 Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive up to **10 paid sick days per year**. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than three consecutive working days. Sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out when employment ends. Employees should notify their manager as early as possible on the first day of absence.

## PTO-8 Emergency and Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to **5 days of paid bereavement leave** following the death of an immediate family member, and up to 2 days for extended family. Emergency leave for urgent family situations may be approved by the manager at short notice and is recorded as PTO unless People Operations agrees otherwise.

## PTO-9 Public Holidays

Halvorsen Analytics observes the public holidays of the country in which the employee is employed. The annual holiday calendar is published in the HR portal each December. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a replacement day to be taken within 60 days.

## PTO-10 Payout on Leaving

When employment ends, accrued but unused PTO, including carried-over days that have not yet expired, is paid out in the final paycheck at the employee's base daily rate. Any PTO taken in advance of accrual is deducted as described in PTO-3.
